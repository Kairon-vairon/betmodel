How a bet updates itself

1
Full time detectedThe results feed is checked every 15 minutes for matches with open bets.
2
Graded by market rulesWon, lost or void (e.g. draw no bet on a draw). Skipped bets are graded too, but don't touch your bankroll.
3
Everything recalculatesBankroll, P/L, ROI, closing line value and calibration update, and the stake limits rescale to the new bankroll.
4
The model learnsThe graded bet joins the training data for the next weekly retrain. You get a summary notification.
Disagree with a grading? Open the bet and use Correct result. Overrides are marked in the log.
